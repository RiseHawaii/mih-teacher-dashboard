What it is

Story-driven SEL, made in Hawaiʻi

Make IT Happen is an 8-module social-emotional learning curriculum for grades 6–9. Each module pairs video case studies of real kamaʻāina with a student workbook and a hands-on project, so students build confidence, identity, and purpose.

Students meet local artists, athletes, entrepreneurs, and changemakers, and see what's possible for themselves right here at home.

Program goals
  • Expand equity and access to future-focused SEL
  • Help students put their personal and professional goals into words
  • Grow mindset, community connection, and self-efficacy
  • Use real local stories to inspire courage and action

How every module runs

The same rhythm, eight different themes

Once you've taught one module, you know the shape of all of them. Each runs 6–7 class sessions of 40–45 minutes.

  1. Day 1Kickoff

    Pre-assessment, intro video, workbook warm-up, and a class discussion.

  2. Day 2Case study 1

    Read the intro, watch the video, then knowledge check and reflection.

  3. Day 3Case study 2

    Same flow with the second kamaʻāina story.

  4. Day 4Case study 3

    Same flow, then compare traits across all three stories.

  5. Day 5Synthesis

    Discussion, catch-up, and the post-assessment.

Days 6–7 (optional): a hands-on project, like a Dream Board, Fear Map, or Mentor Request Letter.

CASEL alignment

Each kamaʻāina story models social-emotional growth in action. Use this guide to connect the stories to the five CASEL competencies across advisory, electives, and core classes.

The five competencies

What students are building

01Self-Awareness

Recognizing your emotions, thoughts, and values, and how they shape what you do. Includes confidence, strengths, and identity.

02Self-Management

Managing emotions, setting goals, controlling impulses, and persisting through challenges.

03Social Awareness

Empathy for others, including people from different backgrounds, and recognizing inequality or injustice.

04Relationship Skills

Building healthy relationships through communication, teamwork, conflict resolution, and asking for or offering help.

05Responsible Decision-Making

Making ethical, constructive choices based on values, consequences, and the well-being of self and others.

Get your class started

Make IT Happen is teacher-facilitated: you lead the lessons and students work in printed workbooks. Here's how to get ready.

  1. Choose your modules

    Every module stands alone. Teach all eight in order, or pick the themes that fit your class and calendar.

    Compare modules →

  2. Get your workbooks

    Each student needs the printed workbook for the module you're teaching.

    To order, email aloha@risehi.com with the modules and number of workbooks you need, and we'll send you a quote.

    Printed workbooks arrive about 1–2 weeks after the invoice is paid.

  3. Set up the tech

    You'll need a screen for videos and slides, and student devices for the pre- and post-assessments.

  4. Prep your first module

    Each module page has videos, slides, a day-by-day plan, and a checklist.

Pacing

How long it takes

Each module runs 6–7 class sessions of 40–45 minutes: five core days plus an optional project. All eight modules add up to roughly 40 core sessions, or about 56 with every project.

Assessments

Program surveys

Every module includes a short pre-assessment on Day 1 and a post-assessment on Day 5. These are RiseHI's program surveys: responses come to us so we can measure and improve Make IT Happen. They aren't graded, and please have every student complete both. Links are on each module page.

Do I have to teach the modules in order?

No. Each module is self-contained, so schools can mix and match.

Do students need their own devices?

Only for the assessments. Videos and slides can be shown on one classroom screen.

What if a video won't play?

Use printed transcripts with the workbook reflections, and let us know so we can fix it.
